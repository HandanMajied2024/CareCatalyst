## If you run late
- Slide 7 is the only long one: skip Eero's first line and the last two lines (saves about 8 s).
- Never skip slide 8: say the last sentence and leave the QR up.

## Wording that must stay (fact check)
- Say **wellbeing services counties** (or just counties), not welfare counties.
- Research partners **could** fund the rest: it is a proposal, and only anonymised, aggregated patterns leave.
- The agent **can't diagnose**, it books a check-up. Kela covers **most of** the taxi.
- Sources on screen: reasons from H. Kuitunen, OUH, Equitable Cancer Care; median age at diagnosis 70.1 / 68.4 (Finnish Cancer Registry data, 2013–2017); 64% of 75–89 year olds online (Statistics Finland 2024). App A shows synthetic patients.

## After 2:00
Leave slide 8 up (QR opens the landing page: For counties = app A, For you = app B). Appendix from slide 9: Data & limits (Q&A), Goal 3 indicator, business case, pricing, four proposals, how it works, live demo, agent to agent, CarePulse plan.
