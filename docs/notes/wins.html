AstraZeneca · Equal Care Across Finland

Cancer care in Finland should be equal and effective wherever people live. Build an AI-powered system that finds the gap, shows where in the pathway it emerges, and says what to do about it.

Pathway
DetectionLate detection: cancer may not be found early enough.
→
ReferralPrimary → specialist care: referrals may be delayed or not progress smoothly.
→
Diagnosis → treatmentPatients may not start treatment within the target timeframes.

The catch AZ stresses

Data on cancer care in Finland is limited and closing that data gap is part of the challenge. Public, aggregate data only, no patient-level data provided or required. Combine public sources with interviews of AZ experts.

Our idea · CareCatalyst

AZ knows the reasons why people don't start cancer treatment and how many are affected, but not who. An opt-in agent follows the pathway, asks why when it stalls, helps, and sends the reasons back aggregated.
