Care Equity pilot brief — West Uusimaa

2024 breast screening participation: 76.1% (22 860 of 30 039 invited).
West Uusimaa would have needed 1 172 more attendees to reach 80%, with the same number invited.

Investigate barriers to participation
Review invitation delivery and participation by locality and language, where reporting permits. Interview invitees to identify barriers worth testing.
Owner — Screening programme lead
Measure — Document baseline participation and priority barriers by subgroup.
