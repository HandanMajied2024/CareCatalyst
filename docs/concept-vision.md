# Concept B: Personal Health Agent (vision)

Repo: [`concepts/vision`](../concepts/vision)

## Idea

Not the county's agent calls me. **My own personal agent** knows my daily life and books care for me, flexibly.

- **Linked to my digital health record** (MyKanta today; under the European Health Data Space patients get the right to share their data with services they choose). I decide what the agent may see.
- **Knows my context**: past treatments, eligibility for support, disabilities, transport limits, budget, daily schedule.
- **Reacts in daily life**: notices symptoms in conversation, connects them with my risk score, suggests a check-up.
- **Acts for me**: books the appointment, applies for Kela transport, talks to institutions.
- **Agent-to-agent**: speaks with the county's Care Pathway Agent (Concept A), the insurer's agent and the doctor's agent.

## The scene (pitch / video)

Character: elderly, visually impaired man with his dog **Jenny**, lives in **Espoo**.

1. Making coffee, talking to his voice assistant about new dog food. Something falls on the floor.
2. He bends down: "ugh", his back hurts.
3. Agent: *"Oh yeah, I've ordered the dog food for Jenny."*
4. Agent: *"By the way, is your back hurting again? You've already been to the orthopedist, and your health risk score has increased to a level where you should get yourself checked out."*
5. He asks where the centre is. Agent: in **Helsinki**.
6. Agent: *"And don't worry about transportation. I know you don't like using public transportation. The Finnish government has a transportation assistance program that you may be eligible for. Your insurance could also cover the transportation costs."*
7. Agent: *"Should I book the appointment for tomorrow at 8 in the morning, before you go to your bingo class?"*
8. He: *"Yes, thank you very much."* He plays with the dog.

**What the agent knows in the scene:** orthopedist visit (health record), eligibility for cancer-related services, visual impairment, public transport is hard, taxi not affordable, daily routine (bingo).

## Closing: agent-to-agent

His agent talks directly to the county's agent from the start of the pitch: requests the slot, confirms the Kela taxi, shares only what's needed. He never has to explain his situation again.
